The Indelible Mark of Democracy

A permanent symbol of your unique contribution to the nation's future.

fingerprint PROCEDURE

Once your ID is confirmed, the Second Polling Officer will apply a vertical line of indelible ink on your left forefinger.

This ink contains silver nitrate, which reacts with skin proteins and sunlight to create a stain that remains visible for several weeks. It's the ultimate safeguard against double voting.

Did you know?

Developed by India's National Physical Laboratory, this "Mark of Democracy" has been used since the 1962 elections. It's now a global standard, exported by Mysore Paints & Varnish Ltd to over 25 countries including Canada and Nigeria.
