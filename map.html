Eligibility to Vote

Before you can cast your vote in the world's largest democracy, you need to ensure you meet the fundamental criteria set by the Constitution of India.

cake

Age Requirement

Must be 18 years or older on the qualifying date of registration.

public

Citizenship

Must be a legal citizen of India. NRIs are eligible under special rules.

quiz

Quick Check

"A person can be registered as a voter in more than one constituency if they own property in multiple states."

tips_and_updates

Correct Answer!

Section 17 of the RPA, 1950 clearly states that no person can be registered in more than one constituency. This ensures the integrity of the democratic process.
